  # 🧠 BrainBoost AI

**BrainBoost AI** is an AI-powered study assistant that helps students turn study materials into **easy-to-understand notes, flashcards, and mind maps**.

Instead of spending hours manually summarizing PDFs or text files, BrainBoost AI uses AI to extract and organize important information into useful study resources.

## ✨ Features

* 📄 **PDF & Text Support** – Upload study materials and extract their content.
* 📝 **AI-Generated Notes** – Convert lengthy content into concise, structured notes.
* 🃏 **Flashcards** – Generate question-and-answer flashcards for revision.
* 🧠 **Mind Maps** – Organize important concepts into a visual-style structure.
* ⚡ **Generate All** – Generate multiple study resources from the same material.
* 🎯 **Student-Friendly Output** – Focuses on important concepts and easy revision.
* 📊 **Usage Statistics** – Tracks application usage.
* 🌐 **Web-Based** – Access the application through a browser.
